PRIVACY

Your information stays on your PC.

Window Lock has no account, analytics, advertising, telemetry, cloud sync, or remote reporting. It sends no user, app, device, or diagnostic information anywhere.

It reads selected Windows settings and limited installation records only to show status. Results are not saved. Local policy backups are stored only so UNPROTECT can safely restore settings Window Lock owns.

Ko-fi, feedback, and documentation links open in your browser only when selected. Nothing is submitted automatically.
